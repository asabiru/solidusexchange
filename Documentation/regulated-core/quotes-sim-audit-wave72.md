# Quotes Simulator Audit — Wave 72 — Proposed

- Status: Proposed
- Scope: `packages/provider-simulators/src/quotes.mjs` (the D-007 EXTERNAL LIQUIDITY stand-in — the exchanger's entire synthetic pricing surface) plus its consumers `miniapp/src/server/provider-quotes.ts`, `miniapp/src/server/server.ts` (`/bff/quotes/preview`) and `miniapp/src/server/quotes.ts` (the local preview source and the contract-quotes adapter). Cross-checked against the upstream mirror `packages/customer-api/src/quotes.mjs` and the consumer validators in `miniapp/src/server/customer-api-client.ts`. Everything is dev-only and synthetic — no production paths, no live data, no execution surface exists anywhere.
- Production effect: none

Hunt list applied: derived-amount representability (the 18-integer-digit canonical wall on outputs, not only inputs); clock consistency between the BFF bucket read, the simulator's internal issue read and the verify/assess window; replayed-quote signature aging against the verifier freshness window; provider error mapping completeness (no non-`ProviderError` escape, no 500); route ordering (upstream availability read vs malformed-input rejection); the amount-grammar seam between the BFF's wider `toUnits` grammar and the provider's canonical grammar; and the standing classes (bounds, canonicalization, first-wins duplicates, deterministic ids, contract coherence, failure surfaces).

## Defects found and fixed

1. **Derived-amount overflow escaped as a raw `RangeError` → 500 (`packages/provider-simulators/src/quotes.mjs`).** Inputs are capped at 18 integer digits by `parsePositiveAmount`, but the *derived* quote-asset amounts are not: a sell of `999999999999999999.000000` USDT prices out to ~20-digit `quote_amount`/`total_quote_amount`, so `formatAmount` threw `RangeError: decimal exceeds the supported integer digits` inside `idempotency.run` — a non-`ProviderError` that bypassed every contract mapping and surfaced at `/bff/quotes/preview` as an unhandled 500 (repro'd). `requestQuote` now rejects with `invalid_request` ("base_amount is too large to quote") when `quoteUnits` or `total` reaches `10^(MAX_INTEGER_DIGITS + quoteScale)`; `fee` needs no separate check because `feeBps ≤ 1000` keeps it dominated by `max(quoteUnits, total)`. `MAX_INTEGER_DIGITS` is now exported from `decimal.mjs` as the contract constant it is (the miniapp checker cannot consume const exports through the `.mjs` `export *` boundary, so the BFF adapter mirrors the value locally with a comment). Pinned: the sell/buy/base-scale arms replay to the identical error and a well-formed request afterwards still issues a quote.
2. **Two clock reads let a preview fail `not_yet_valid` on a one-second tick (`miniapp/src/server/provider-quotes.ts`).** The adapter read `now` once for the idempotency bucket and reused it for `verify`/`assessQuote`, while the simulator re-read `clock.now()` inside `requestQuote`. A forward tick between the reads minted a quote at `issued_at = T+1` assessed at `T` → `not_yet_valid` → `quote_unavailable`/`invalid_quote` (repro'd with a clock advancing one second per read). Verification and assessment now use `verifiedNow = max(nowSeconds(), floor(issued_at))` — the post-issue instant — while the bucket keeps the pre-request `now`. Honest checks are unaffected: `future_timestamp` cannot trip (`verifiedNow ≥ issued`), `stale_timestamp` still detects a genuinely aged replay, `expired` and `stale_price` still assess at the same instant.
3. **Malformed previews spent an upstream wallet read and could answer 503 (`miniapp/src/server/server.ts`).** `previewAvailability` ran *before* `quoteProvider.preview` validated the pair and amount, so a verified session with a configured customer-api sent `from=RUB&to=RUB`, `amount=abc` or `amount=0` through `customerApi.wallets` — and an upstream outage answered `503 quote_unavailable` where the contract is `400 invalid_pair`/`invalid_amount` (repro'd against a stub that records calls). The route now runs the provider-agnostic grammar first — asset codes, `from !== to` (equivalent to the routable pair set for both providers), `toUnits` parseable and `> 0` — before any upstream call. Provider-specific bounds (the 18-digit wall) stay with the provider. Pinned: malformed inputs return 400 with zero wallet calls; a well-formed preview still prices against served balances exactly once.
4. **19–30-digit amounts were mislabeled `amount_too_small` (`miniapp/src/server/provider-quotes.ts`).** The BFF `toUnits` grammar allows 30 integer digits while the provider's canonical grammar stops at 18, so `amount=99999999999999999999` (or any value past the wall) parsed locally, failed `parsePositiveAmount` downstream and surfaced as `invalid_request` → `amount_too_small` — a grammar violation reported as a dust quote (repro'd). `parseAmount` now throws `invalid_amount` for `units >= 10^(18 + scale)` before the provider call. The two grammar layers are now: route-level (canonical decimal, positive) and provider-level (canonical decimal, representable).
5. **A replayed quote could age out of the signature freshness window → 503 (`miniapp/src/server/provider-quotes.ts`, latent).** `exportSignedQuote` re-signs the stored quote at its original `issued_at`; under `expired_quote` (issue offset −120s) at `ttlSeconds = 300` — allowed by `createQuoteSimulator` though unreachable through the shipped config (MINIAPP_QUOTE_TTL_SECONDS is pinned 10–120) — an in-bucket replay at T+181 verified `stale_timestamp` → `quote_unavailable` where the contract answer is the same preview (repro'd bucket-aligned). The adapter now re-issues once under a salted idempotency key (`{bucket}:reissue`) and re-verifies the fresh signature rather than lying about the signature timestamp; a second failure still fails closed.

## Verified clean (with negative pins)

- **Price/rate math is exact and honest**: `price = mid·(20000 ∓ spreadBps)/20000` on bigint at `PRICE_SCALE = 8`, `quoteUnits = base·price·10^quoteScale/10^(baseScale+8)`, fee and total likewise — no float anywhere in the chain. Rounding is always customer-unfavourable (`up` on buy, `down` on sell, fee always `up`) and the `rounding` field reports the true direction; `validateSignedQuote` recomputes every field and rejects any drift. The upstream mirror `computeAmounts` is byte-identical logic on the same reference book.
- **Fee semantics**: `fee = ceil(quoteUnits·feeBps/10000)` in the quote asset, `fee_asset: "quote_asset"` pinned by the validator; displayed `fee_amount`/`fee_bps` are the charged values — the adapter surfaces them verbatim.
- **TTL/validity gating is real**: `assessQuote` orders `not_yet_valid` < `expired` < `stale_price` < displayable against an injected `now`; `expires_at = issued_at + ttl_seconds` is pinned inside the quote payload. Nothing anywhere is redeemable: `execution: "not_supported"` is pinned at mint, verified after transport, enforced by contract validators upstream, and there is no accept/execute route — `ExchangeScreen` renders a `DisabledCta` and `recordQuote` only logs a bounded preview event.
- **Pair/amount gating is complete**: only the three declared pairs; direction maps `sell`/`buy` exactly once; `from === to` is `invalid_pair`; zero/negative/non-decimal/dust inputs are `invalid_amount`/`amount_too_small`; `quote_amount` mode is buy-only and exclusive.
- **Determinism within a TTL window**: the idempotency key digests `[subject, pair, side, base_amount, quote_amount, bucket]` — same inputs replay the same stored quote and quote id; a new bucket mints a fresh one; seeded `random` keeps the whole stream reproducible.
- **Bounds and canonicalization**: quotes map, idempotency registry and nonce store each bounded at 10k per generation; a new generation every 5,000 previews bounds memory; `snapshotRequest` rejects unknown/extra/duplicate fields; `searchParams.get` is first-wins for duplicates; the route allowlist rejects unknown params `400 invalid_request`.
- **Contract coherence**: preview quotes carry `status: "indicative"` pinned and re-checked post-verify; the simulated mid stays within the same ±25 bps envelope the upstream contract quotes use (same reference mids 90/300/3.2, digest jitter upstream vs seeded jitter in the simulator); the adapter re-verifies the delivered signature envelope and field-matches `quote_id`/`pair`/`side`/`status`/`execution` before anything renders.
- **Failure surfaces**: every malformed request maps to `invalid_pair`/`invalid_amount`/`amount_too_small` (400) or `quote_unavailable` with `provider_outage`/`stale_price`/`invalid_quote` (503); no fabricated rates; no silent fallback to local math on outage.

## Observations (recorded, not defects in this slice)

- **Two independent books exist by design**: the default `quoteSource: "local"` preview prices off `midPricesRub` (USDT 92.40, TON 285.10) — a *different* synthetic book than the contract/simulator reference (USDT 90, TON 300/3.2) — so the ExchangeScreen's default live rate and the `/bff/quotes` collection do not describe the same market. `quoteSource: "provider-simulator"` shares the contract book. The local model also charges its fee in the `from` asset pre-deduction while the simulator charges in the quote asset post-computation — two honest, different conventions.
- **Preview and collection id grammars differ by surface**: `quote_<32hex>` (simulator), `Q-*` (local), `qte_<24hex>` (contract collection); preview ids never appear in `/bff/quotes`. Same for timestamp precision (second ISO vs millisecond ISO upstream).
- **Expired quotes are intentionally returned to the client** (assessment `reason === "expired"` passes through) so the screen can render the expired state; they remain non-executable everywhere.
- **Price staleness is assessed at issue** (`issued − observed ≤ 60`), not at view — a replayed quote keeps its original observation age; the TTL, not `price_observed_at`, bounds how long it is displayable.
- **The nonce store sweeps all expired entries on every `record`** — O(n) per insert, worst-case quadratic within a generation; bounded at 10k entries / 5k previews per generation, acceptable at dev scale.
- **`getQuote`/`exportSignedQuote` stay readable after expiry** — display gating lives in `assessQuote`, not the store; consumers must always assess.
