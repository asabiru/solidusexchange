# Provider Simulators Adversarial Audit — Wave 58 — Proposed

- Status: Proposed
- Scope: `packages/provider-simulators/` as a unit — the dev-only simulated providers (`kyc.mjs`, `kyt.mjs`, `bank.mjs`, `quotes.mjs`, `checks.mjs`) plus the shared machinery (`simulator-core.mjs`, `callback-inbox.mjs`, `signing.mjs`, `canonical-json.mjs`, `deterministic.mjs`, `decimal.mjs`, `errors.mjs`, `interfaces.mjs`) and how miniapp BFF and backoffice consume them through signature envelopes and the callback inbox. Wave 38 covered internals; this wave re-audited the package end to end as the producer of every trusted envelope, with explicit hunts on state-machine integrity, envelope/signing hygiene, callback machinery, determinism/isolation, input robustness, bounds and fail-closed surfaces.
- Production effect: none

## Defects found and fixed

1. **The KYC consumer state machine admitted skipping the review step.** `KYC_TRANSITIONS` let a single sequence-1 callback move an application from `submitted` straight to a decision, although in every flow this simulator emits the first event is always `in_review` — a first callback is never `approved`, `rejected` or `needs_more_data`. The table was wider than the contract it defends; `submitted` now transitions only into `in_review`, and terminal re-entry plus repeated transitions stay refused.
2. **The KYT consumer state machine admitted skipping the evaluation step.** `KYT_TRANSITIONS` let a sequence-1 `completed` callback deliver a finished screening (`risk_level`, `risk_score`, `sanctions_hit`) with no `pending` ever observed — again wider than anything this simulator emits. `requested` now transitions only into `pending`.
3. **Exported scenario fixtures were frozen only at the surface.** `KYC_SCENARIOS`, `KYT_SCENARIOS`, `BANK_SCENARIOS`, `QUOTE_SCENARIOS` and `CHECKS_SCENARIOS` wrapped their inner plan objects (and `BANK_SCENARIOS.*.events` arrays) with no `Object.freeze`, so any importer could mutate shared policy — e.g. turn `KYT_SCENARIOS.low`'s risk into `severe` or push events onto a bank plan — and every later simulator instance would emit the mutated behavior: the shared-object-mutation class under determinism/isolation. All fixture objects, their nested arrays and the module-private constant lists (`RISK_SCORE_RANGES`, `RISK_CATEGORIES`, status/level/pair/key lists) are now frozen through.
4. **`FORBIDDEN_OPERATION` matched verb prefixes case-sensitively.** An adapter exposing `Payout`, `Execute`, `Settle`, `transferOwner`, `WithdrawAll` or `PayOut` passed `assertProviderAdapter` — only exact-lowercase spellings were refused. The verb alternation is now case-insensitive while the word-boundary lookahead stays case-sensitive, and names that are all-caps (`PAYOUT_TIMEOUT_MS`, `SIGNATURE_HEADERS`) remain constants by convention and out of scope.
5. **Every per-resource store grew without bound** (the #281 class, promoted from a wave-38 observation now that bounds are in scope): `IdempotencyRegistry.entries`, `DeliveryQueue.pending`, inbox `subjects`/`eventIds`, and each simulator's domain map (`timelines`, `assessments`, `intents`, `quotes`, `checks`). All are now bounded and fail closed instead of evicting or growing: the registry and queue take `maxEntries`/`maxPending` (default 10 000; `capacity_exceeded` is a new `ProviderError` code), each simulator takes `maxResources` (default 10 000) applied to its domain map, idempotency registries and pending-callback budget (16× resources, above the worst per-resource emit fan-out), and `createCallbackInbox` takes `maxSubjects` (refused outright) and `maxEventIds` (held for review as `invalid_transition`). A refused create leaves no partial state behind.
6. **`reviewTimeoutSeconds` (KYC) and `screeningTimeoutSeconds` (KYT) had floors but no ceilings.** An absurd value either produced an absurd deadline (a ~year-9999 `review_deadline`) or, past the epoch bound, surfaced as a `RangeError` deep inside the idempotent create — after the timeline and its scheduled callbacks had already been registered, leaking a half-created resource no caller can see or discard. Both options are now bounded at construction (600–604 800 s for KYC review, 60–86 400 s for KYT screening) and the deadline serialization is computed before any store mutation.
7. **The failed-claim counter on a check grew without bound.** Every wrong `claim_reference` incremented `failed_claim_attempts` forever — an online oracle counting attempts with no consequence. Past 25 failures the check now locks: further claims fail closed with `invalid_request` before the digest is even evaluated, the counter stops growing, and the check resolves only via cancel or expiry.

## Verified clean

- **State-machine declarations beyond the skip paths**: `approved` is not re-enterable, nor are `rejected`, `completed`, `claimed`, `cancelled`, `expired`, `payment_reversed` and `expired_no_payment` — every terminal transition set is empty; a next-sequence event carrying a status already applied is `invalid_transition`; buffering precedes the transition check so out-of-order delivery resolves to the legal cascade, and replayed `event_id`s are `duplicate`, while the same `event_id` with different content is `conflict`.
- **Envelope/signing hygiene**: the callback signer refuses payloads of another domain or `environment` and signs a deep-copied canonical body at schedule time — mutating the payload afterwards cannot change the emitted envelope. Validators pin the exact member set, so a forged-but-authentic callback carrying an extra PII-shaped member (`passport_number`, `raw_address`) fails `invalid_payload`; the verifier re-serializes the body byte-identically before payload checks (wave-38 and #258 chain).
- **Callback machinery**: delivery is pull-based and deterministic — same seed and key give byte-identical transcripts (already pinned by `conformance.test.mjs` and `determinism.test.mjs`); per-subject dedup records are now bounded (defect 5) and `parked` buffers are bounded by the 1–1000 sequence space.
- **Determinism/isolation**: `snapshotRequest` refuses non-plain objects, accessors, symbols and unknown members; scenario resolution is `Object.hasOwn` over a frozen null-prototype map, so `__proto__`/`constructor` names cannot resolve; one subject's resources are unreachable through another's references (`not_found` on unguessable `SIM_ID` handles).
- **Input robustness**: malformed bodies are refused by `snapshotRequest` before any state exists; malformed `receivedAt`/`sequence`/event content is held for review, never applied (wave-38 chain); NaN/Infinity cannot reach `toIsoSeconds`/`assertEpochSeconds`; subject ids must be non-empty strings — delimiter smuggling is a non-object value, refused.
- **Fail-closed surfaces**: every validator runs on the canonical body after authentication and before state mutation; staleness/TTL windows compare epoch seconds from the injected clock and cannot be bypassed by crafted `now` strings in payloads (payload times must be ISO-seconds in range); all emitted identifiers are `sim-`/SIM_ID synthetic, assets and networks are testnet-only (`TON_TESTNET`, `TRON_TESTNET`, `RUB`/`sbp`), `providerId` is pinned to `simulator`, `environment` to `dev-simulator`, and `assertProviderAdapter` still refuses real-provider-shaped ids and money-moving operation names — now also in mixed casing (defect 4).

## Observations (recorded, not defects in this slice)

- Late and stale events still consume one dedup record each before classification; bounded by `maxEventIds` (default 1 024) and the 1–1000 sequence space rather than eliminated, since the record is needed to detect replayed ids. At the bound, further distinct ids are `invalid_transition`, not silently dropped.
- All-caps operation names are treated as constants by convention and stay out of `FORBIDDEN_OPERATION`'s scope (so `SIGNATURE_HEADERS` cannot trip on `sign`); a callable adapter member named in all caps is outside the casing model rather than refused — covered by the static source scan and recorded here.
- `awaiting_recipient_kyc` is a dead-end for unverifiable members by design (member directory is immutable); only `cancelled`/`expired` resolve such checks.
- The provider-side queue has no per-subject cancellation: discarded consumers surface provider deliveries as `unknown_subject`, which already fails closed; `maxPending` bounds total growth.
- `check.sequence` increments on emits that produce no callback (`silent_callback`), so sequences may skip on the consumer side — consistent per-check scenario behavior, not corruption.
- The static source scanner (`scripts/check-boundary.mjs`) matches money-moving operation names case-sensitively, the same class as defect 4 at the runtime boundary; left as an observation for the tooling slice rather than widened here.

## Test additions

- `packages/provider-simulators/tests/machinery-audit-wave58.test.mjs` (16 cases): sequence-1 decision callbacks refused for KYC and KYT — never `approved`, `rejected`, `needs_more_data` or `completed` as a first transition — while the legal `in_review`/`pending` then outcome cascades still apply; transition tables pinned to the review-mandatory shapes; terminal re-entry and repeated transitions refused; deep-freeze pins on every exported fixture object and nested array plus a post-mutation-attempt emission check; case-confused forbidden operations (`Payout`, `Execute`, `Settle`, `transferOwner`, `WithdrawAll`, `PayOut`, `SubmitOrder`, `Trade`) refused while constants and noun forms stay allowed; capacity bounds on `IdempotencyRegistry`, `DeliveryQueue`, all five domain stores and the inbox (`maxSubjects`, `maxEventIds`) — refused creates leave no pending callbacks; timeout option ceilings refused at construction with no half-created resource; the 25-attempt failed-claim bound locking the check with the counter frozen; sign-time snapshot and domain/environment signer pinning.
