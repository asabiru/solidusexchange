# Demo/Degrade Oracles Audit — Wave 74 — Proposed

- Status: Proposed
- Production effect: none
- Scope: `backoffice/src/data/demo.ts` (the `demoRepository` that powers the backoffice degrade path and the e2e-dev fixtures) and `miniapp/src/server/{payments,cards,deposits,withdrawals}.ts` plus the shared `syntheticData` (the miniapp's local/gated synthetic universe), against the contract pins the fail-closed adapters assert — the check and withdrawal status enums, the custody `maximum_intent_ttl_seconds: 300` / pending `approval_ttl_seconds: 900` windows, the 30 bps check-fee rail, the id grammars, and the cross-resource link fabric. Everything is dev-only and synthetic.

Hunt list applied: contract fidelity of every emitted field vs the enums/grammars/scales the adapters assert fail-closed; schema drift between the hand-copied demo literals and the waves 71–72 strict pins; determinism (wall-clock freedom, digest-id collision-freedom); coverage asymmetry (empty degrade surfaces, happy-path-only rows); cross-oracle consistency (backoffice demo vs miniapp synthetic vs e2e stack); leak surface (demo data on a live path or live data shared-mutated into demo).

## Defects found and fixed

1. **The demo check status enum had drifted off the contract.** `CheckStatus` used the kebab-case literal `waiting-recipient-kyc` where the check contract pins `awaiting_recipient_kyc` — the BFF status allowlist, the `server-fields` map and all three locale bundles copied the drifted literal. A served check row in that status is one the strict adapters would never emit, so the degrade surface taught the operator UI a fiction. The type, fixture row (CHK-771298), `/bff/api/checks` status filter, server-fields map and en/ru/ky labels now carry the contract token.
2. **The demo withdrawal enum invented seven states; the contract has ten.** `WithdrawalIntentStatus` pinned `pending-approval` (kebab, single step) plus a reduced set, while the custody contract's canonical list is `draft`, `screened`, `pending_maker_approval` is pending maker, `pending_checker_approval` is pending checker, `unsigned_intent_ready`, `broadcast`, `confirmed`, `rejected`, `cancelled`, `expired`. The BFF's `withdrawalStatuses` allowlist would have rejected live statuses like `unsigned_intent_ready` on the live path as well, and the UI had no label for them. All ten statuses are now the enum, the BFF filter admits each one, `server-fields` and locales gained the missing labels, and the fixture rows re-map onto contract states (WDR-991804 now reads `pending_checker_approval` — pending checker after a recorded maker decision).
3. **Intent lifecycles ignored the 300-second custody TTL.** Every `withdrawalIntent` row kept `expiresAt − createdAt` well under 300 s but recorded timeline events and maker/checker decisions *after* `expiresAt` (repro'd: WDR-991804's checker state and WDR-991788's checker decision landed minutes past expiry), and each pending intent was already expired relative to the demo universe's own latest recorded instant — degrade mode showed expired intents as actionable pending work. All seven rows were rebased so `expiresAt = createdAt + 300 s` and every pre-resolution event/decision lands inside the window; `withdrawal.confirmed` may legitimately arrive after the signing window closes, and a `withdrawal.expired` record lands exactly at `expiresAt`.
4. **The miniapp synthetic universe had an already-expired pending withdrawal.** `wdr_f1e2d3c4b5a6f7e8d9c0b1a2` sat in `pending_maker_approval` is pending with `expiresAt` before the synthetic universe's latest recorded instant — the same lie as (3), one oracle over. Rebased to `2026-10-08T09:20:00Z` so it stays live inside the synthetic "now".
5. **A pending-approval coverage hole hid three contract statuses.** Once the real enum was wired, the app test that requires a fixture row per documented status failed on `unsigned_intent_ready`, `expired`, and `pending_maker_approval` is pending. Three intents were added — WDR-991830 (pending maker decision, freshest row), WDR-991840 (both decision steps recorded, unsigned intent awaiting broadcast), WDR-991820 (expired before any decision) — which also strengthens the deny/edge coverage this audit hunts for.
6. **`kycCaseId` pointed at KYC cases that do not exist.** CUS-10475 carried `kycCaseId: "KYC-220171"` and CUS-10468 `kycCaseId: "KYC-220168"` — neither id exists in `kycCases`, so a subject-detail join in degrade mode dangled. The links were removed and the field made optional, matching the customer shape.
7. **The gated-recipient story pointed at a verified recipient.** The support ticket SUP-384120 ("recipient cannot accept the check") belonged to a customer who was neither sender nor recipient of the only `awaiting_recipient_kyc` check. CHK-771298 now names Алина (CUS-10482 — the ticket owner, verified) as sender and Никита (CUS-10477 — whose EDD case KYC-220177 is still open) as the gated recipient; the ticket's claim is true.
8. **Check fees were off the canonical 30 bps rail.** Five demo checks carried fees that did not equal `ceil(amount·30/10000)` at the asset scale (e.g. `0.050000` on `25.000000` USDT where the rail charges `0.075000`). Degrade mode would quote fees the live contract never computes. All five fee fields were recomputed ceil-exact.
9. **Queue totals and the open-cases metric contradicted the rows they summarize.** "High-risk withdrawals" advertised `total: 4` while six intents are non-terminal (now incl. the new pending-maker/unsigned/broadcast rows); "AML alerts" said 7 against 3 cases; "KYC review" said 18 against 3; the open-cases metric said 29 against 13 enumerable open cases. Degrade dashboards now reconcile with the enumerable fixtures.
10. **Audit ordering and pending-approval queue references drifted.** AUD-000148/149 sat at the head of the append-only log while describing events that occurred later than entries after them; they were renumbered to AUD-000156/157 at the tail, postdating the WDR-991804 lifecycle they record. APV-843921 referenced `withdrawal:WDL-991804` — a grammar that no intent emits — now `withdrawal:WDR-991804`, with its age recomputed to the rebased timestamps.

## Verified clean (with negative pins)

- **Determinism**: no wall-clock, RNG or environment reads exist anywhere in `demo.ts` or the miniapp synthetic modules — every timestamp is an absolute ISO literal and every id a fixed literal, so the same boot serves the same universe; the TTL checks evaluate each universe against its own latest recorded instant rather than wall time.
- **Leak surface**: the backoffice BFF reaches `demoRepository` only inside the degrade/unconfigured branches — upstream reads run first and fall back on non-`ok` — and the miniapp synthetic modules sit behind the dev gate; e2e-dev consumes `demoRepository` deliberately from `.server-dist`. No live path serves demo data and nothing writes back into the fixture store.
- **Id grammars**: backoffice ids match the asserted patterns (`CHK-\d{6}`, `WDR-\d{6}`, `SUP-\d{6}`, `KYC-\d{6}`, `AML-\d{5}`, `AUD-\d{6}`, `APV-\d{6}`, `CUS-\d{5}`, `sim-*`, `operator_ref_*`, `custody_intent_*`, `custody_idempotency_*`, `destination_ref_[a-z0-9_]{2,32}`); miniapp synthetic ids match its pins (`chk_`/`dep_`/`wdr_`/`wdl_`/`pay_`/`crd_` 24-hex, `SIMSBP[0-9A-F]{12}`, `SIMBANK[0-9A-F]{16}`) — the new tests pin both grammars.
- **Cross-link fabric**: every `kycCaseId`, `linkedCheckId` (+ sender match), `linkedKycCaseId`, `linkedScreeningId`, `linkedApprovalId`, `relatedAlert/CaseIds`, `linkedInvestigationId` and the pending-approval `resource` now resolves to a fixture row — pinned by a dedicated test.
- **Coverage**: checks cover all five demo statuses (created, `awaiting_recipient_kyc`, claimed, cancelled, expired) and intents cover all ten contract states; KYC cases include verified, review and need-info rows, AML cases include escalated rows — the degrade surfaces render non-happy-path work.

## Observations (recorded, not defects in this slice)

- **The demo "now" is the latest recorded instant in each universe, not wall-clock**: the fixtures freeze their own time; the new tests evaluate expiry and TTL honesty against that internal instant, so they stay deterministic forever.
- **Upstream-only statuses stay absent by design**: the check contract's upstream-only `awaiting_confirmation` has no demo row — the oracle cannot fabricate a state it does not own; recorded here rather than covered.
- **The two oracles use different id namespaces by design**: `CHK-*`/`WDR-*` are ops-side aggregates while `chk_*`/`wdr_*` are the customer-side contract ids the miniapp asserts; a shared subject (CUS-10482) appears through both grammars consistently — different services' ids, not drift.
- **`broadcast` is counted as a live queue item but exempt from the signing-window check**: it is in-flight awaiting rail confirmation, so it legitimately outlives `expiresAt` while remaining non-terminal for the queue tally — the test keeps the two terminal sets distinct.
- **Demo types remain hand-copied literals, not derived contract types**: the drift risk this audit exists for is structural; the new oracle suites are now the machine-checked contract between the fixtures and the adapter pins, which is the honest fix at dev-oracle scope (generating fixtures from the contract schema would exceed this wave's boundary).
