# Bank & Checks Simulators Audit — Wave 73 — Proposed

- Status: Proposed
- Scope: `packages/provider-simulators/src/bank.mjs` (the D-004 SIMSBP deposit rail — the synthetic bank the e2e stack and miniapp MoneyFlow history derive from) and `packages/provider-simulators/src/checks.mjs` (the check-book surface: Telegram-check style instruments), plus the shared receiver `callback-inbox.mjs` that both rails' callback test surfaces sit on. Cross-checked against the consumer pins in `packages/customer-api/src/{deposits,checks,payments}.mjs`, `miniapp/src/app/money-flow.ts` and `miniapp/src/server/checks.ts`. Everything is dev-only and synthetic — no production paths, no live data, no money movement exists anywhere.
- Production effect: none

Hunt list applied: money math (bigint decimal-exactness at RUB=2/USDT=6/TON=9, derived-total representability against the 18-integer-digit canonical wall); lifecycle/state machines (6 deposit statuses, the full checks transition graph, terminal-state resurrection, idempotent re-delivery); determinism and id derivation; bounds (per-subject maps, registries, queue, inbox); check-book specifics (issue/hold/claim/cancel/expire, double-spend, redemption binding, expiry edges); adversarial inputs (malformed refs, duplicate and out-of-order callbacks, negative/zero/dust/oversized amounts); and consumers' pinned assumptions (status vocabularies, id/ref grammars, expiry-coherence rules the adapters assert fail-closed).

## Defects found and fixed

1. **A scenario's `received_total` could overflow the canonical wall after creation (`src/bank.mjs`).** `parsePositiveAmount` bounds the *input* to 18 integer digits, but `received_total` is the sum of every non-reversal leg the plan emits — `duplicate_payment` on `999999999999999999.99` RUB created the intent, scheduled both legs, and `getPaymentStatus` then threw a bare `RangeError` computing the total (repro'd: intent persisted, read crashed with a non-`ProviderError`). `createPaymentIntent` now evaluates the scenario's observed total up front and fails `invalid_request` ("amount is too large to report for this scenario") — the request is refused before any intent or callback exists. Pinned: the overflowing create is refused with zero scheduled callbacks, and a `499999999999999999.99` duplicate still reports `999999999999999999.98`.
2. **In-window bank events could occur after `expires_at` (`src/bank.mjs`).** Event times were `previousAt + 20..240s` with no bound against the intent's validity: `reversed_payment` on a 300s TTL observed `payment_reversed` 75 seconds *after* expiry (repro'd on seed-1), and `payment_received` lands past expiry on TTLs under ~4 minutes — a bank reporting it took money on an intent that had already expired. Ordinary events now clamp to `expiresAt - 1`; the post-expiry observation stays the exclusive domain of the `late_callback` and `payment_not_found` (`expired_no_payment`) scenarios, which keep their deliberate `expiresAt + 60..600` / `+1s` timing. Pinned: across six in-window scenarios × three seeds at a 60s TTL every emitted `occurred_at` is strictly inside the window, and both late-style scenarios still arrive after expiry.
3. **A check could resolve exactly at `expires_at` (`src/checks.mjs`).** `expireIfDue` guarded with `now <= expiresAt`, so at the boundary second the check was still open: a `claimCheck` issued at exactly TTL elapsed succeeded with `resolved_at === expires_at` (repro'd) — colliding with the expired state, which is the only resolution allowed to sit *at* expiry, and breaking the expiry-coherence pin the upstream mirror asserts (claimed/cancelled resolve strictly inside the open window). The guard is now `now < expiresAt`: `expires_at` is the first invalid instant, claims and cancels at the boundary answer `check is expired`, and a claim one second earlier still resolves inside the window.
4. **`previewCheck` quoted terms for checks `createCheck` can never mint (`src/checks.mjs`).** The preview validated refs, amount and asset but skipped both sender gates: an unverified sender and a self-check both priced successfully (repro'd). A preview now enforces `sender.kyc_verified` and `sender_ref !== recipient_ref` exactly as `createCheck` does — the preview can no longer promise an impossible check. Pinned: both gates reject; a well-formed preview still returns its `chkref_` reference.
5. **`awaiting_recipient_kyc → created` was unreachable (`src/checks.mjs`).** The member directory was frozen at construction, so a check parked on the recipient's KYC could only ever cancel or expire — the promote arm in `CHECKS_TRANSITIONS` was dead, and the documented flow (recipient verifies, then claims) could not complete. The simulator now exposes a `setMemberKyc` test hook (the stand-in for the KYC notification a real deployment would deliver) and `promoteIfCleared` performs a lazy, emitted `awaiting_recipient_kyc → created` refresh inside `issueCheck`/`claimCheck`/`cancelCheck`/`getCheckStatus`, mirroring how expiry already refreshes. Pinned: a parked check promotes and claims end-to-end (`awaiting_confirmation → awaiting_recipient_kyc → created → claimed`), an unverified claimant is still refused, and a parked check still cancels.
6. **A callback could claim an event that occurred after expiry (`src/checks.mjs`).** `validateChecksCallback` checked timestamp shape but not coherence: `occurred_at > expires_at` passed (repro'd) — a self-contradictory claim the receiver would order against real events. The validator now rejects any event occurring strictly after the check's expiry; the expired event itself, emitted exactly *at* `expires_at`, remains the only boundary occurrence.
7. **A conflicting claim on a resolved sequence was silently `stale` (`src/callback-inbox.mjs`).** Any event claiming a sequence number already applied — or already buffered — returned `stale` regardless of the status it claimed: a second `claimed`-vs-`created` assertion about seq 1 was dropped with no review flag (repro'd on both the generic and checks inboxes). The subject now records the applied status per sequence (a map bounded by the existing sequence bound of 1000); a claim matching the recorded status stays `stale`, a differing one is `invalid_transition` and counted for review — first claim wins, conflicts are never silent. `boundary.test.mjs` re-pins the semantics: the conflicting buffered-sequence claim is held for review and `reviewEvents` reflects it.

## Verified clean (with negative pins)

- **Money math is exact and honest**: every amount flows as bigint minor units through `parsePositiveAmount`/`formatAmount` — no float anywhere; malformed amounts (`-1.00`, `0`, `0.001`, `abc`, `1e2`, ` 1.00`, 19-integer-digit) all reject `invalid_request`; partial payments keep `received_total < expected` at exact scale.
- **Idempotency is exact-replay**: `IdempotencyRegistry` stores only successful responses and replays them byte-identically; the same key with a different request answers `idempotency_conflict`; a failed claim attempt consumes no registry entry, which keeps wrong-reference retries honest against `MAX_FAILED_CLAIM_ATTEMPTS`.
- **Double-spend and redemption binding hold**: the claim digest is compared with `timingSafeEqual` and only the digest is stored (the claim reference never persists); only the named recipient can claim, only the sender can issue/cancel; a second claim answers `check is claimed`; wrong references increment `failed_claim_attempts` and the 25-attempt lock holds.
- **Determinism is total**: seeded `createSeededRandom` drives every id (`dep_`/`chk_`/`chkevt_`/`chkref_`/`SIMBANK`/`SIMSBP` grammars) and every event delay; `createSimulatedClock` is the only clock — no wall-clock anywhere; same seed reproduces the identical stream.
- **Bounds are complete**: intents/checks maps, idempotency registries, the delivery queue, inbox subjects/parked/event-ids and the verifier nonce store are all capacity-bounded; the new per-sequence `applied` map inherits the `isPositiveSequence` bound (≤ 1000 per subject).
- **Consumer pins hold**: the 6 deposit statuses, `dep_[0-9a-f]{32}`-shaped ids and `SIMSBP[0-9A-F]{12}`/`SIMBANK[0-9A-F]{16}` refs match what `customer-api` deposits/payments and `money-flow.ts` assert; the check `resolved_at`/`expires_at` coherence rules the mirror checks now hold at the boundary too; `posting: "none"` on both surfaces pins that neither adapter moves money.
- **Callback envelope stays strict**: exact-keys payloads, per-status paid/related rules, the signature + canonical-JSON + nonce verification chain, and late/duplicate/out_of_order delivery are unchanged; `expireIfDue`/`promoteIfCleared` only ever emit transitions the transition table declares legal.

## Observations (recorded, not defects in this slice)

- **`comment` is validated but intentionally discarded** — `checkComment` bounds it (string ≤ 140) and nothing stores or emits it, matching the check-book surface that carries no memo field. A future memo needs a schema change, not a store.
- **`chk_<32hex>` sim ids vs `chk_<24hex>` contract-mirror grammar** — the sim's `SIM_ID` grammar is `[a-z]+_[0-9a-f]{32}` while the upstream mirror pins 24 hex; the two surfaces never exchange ids (the sim is self-contained) so this is a documented asymmetry, not a mismatch.
- **`getPaymentStatus`/`getCheckStatus` differ on malformed ids by design** — checks enforce the `CHECK_ID` grammar (`invalid_request`), bank answers `not_found` for anything unknown; both fail closed and neither leaks state.
- **Lazy refresh semantics**: expiry and KYC promotion are observed inside the next operation rather than by a background sweep — a `silent`/outage delivery just never surfaces the emitted transition. Callback `sequence` counts state transitions (each also emits a callback unless the scenario suppresses it), now documented on the typedef.
- **`partial_payment` has no top-up event** — `BANK_TRANSITIONS` lets it go only to `payment_reversed`, matching a rail where a short payment cannot be completed later; the partial leg stays a partial forever unless reversed.
- **Provider-side probes are asymmetric**: `issueCheck` cannot pre-flight an unknown check (existence probe costs an emit-free refresh only), while `previewCheck` mints a reference `createCheck` never binds — the preview reference is a quote token, not a reservation.
